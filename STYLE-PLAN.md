# STYLE-PLAN.md — Map of Reality (Milestone S0 Discovery)

This document maps every concept referenced in the **Style Packs (Recipe v3.2)** specification to the actual files, symbols, signatures, and call sites in the **Recipe Studio** (`v3.1`) repository.

| Concept | File | Symbol / Signature | Notes & Call Sites |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recipe Document & Theme AST** | `src/core/types.ts` | `RecipeDocument`, `ThemeSpec` (`{ style?: string; palette?: number; hex?: string }`), `StyleToken` (`'card' \| 'hero' \| 'disc' \| 'hdisc' \| 'warnp'`), `CompiledRecipe` | `doc.recipe` is `'v3.1'`. `doc.theme` is read in `compileRecipe` (`src/core/layout.ts`), `flattenRecipeDocument` (`src/core/layout.ts`), `validateRecipe` (`src/core/validate.ts`), and `buildPromptForPart` (`src/core/promptbuilder.ts`). |
| **Palette & Token Expansion** | `src/core/palette.ts` | `PALETTE_TABLE`, `PALETTE_NAMES`, `THEME_TOKEN_NAMES`, `expandThemeTokens(theme?: ThemeSpec): ThemeTokens`, `resolveColorToken`, `resolveFillValue`, `hexToRgb`, `rgbToHex`, `rgbToHsl`, `hslToHex` | `PALETTE_TABLE[1..5]` holds the 22 neon tokens (`bg, dark, scn, acc, ink, muted, dim, track, surf, second, cardHi, cardLo, cardS, heroHi, heroLo, discHi, discLo, hdiscHi, hdiscLo, warn, warnpHi, warnpLo`). Called in `compileRecipe`, `flattenRecipeDocument`, `TranscriptPromptScreen.tsx`, and tests. `expandThemeTokens` becomes the legacy wrapper calling `resolveTokens(theme?.style ?? 'neon', theme)`. |
| **Surface Styles Expansion (Compile-Time)** | `src/core/styles.ts` | `STYLE_TOKEN_NAMES`, `expandStyleToken(style)`, `applyStyleToLayer(layer)` | Currently expands `layer.style` (`card`, `hero`, `disc`, `hdisc`, `warnp`) into default `fill`, `stroke`, `strokeWidth`, `radius`, `shadow`, `glow` in `compileRecipe` (`src/core/layout.ts`). Style pack types (`StylePack`, `SurfaceStyle`, `TokenRule`, `GlowPolicy`, `FontRef`), registry (`registerStylePack`, `getStylePack`, `listStylePacks`), `resolveTokens`, and `checkContrast` will live here and in `src/core/styles/*.ts`. |
| **Surface Drawing & Hard-Coded Surface Look** | `src/render/shapes.ts`, `src/render/fx.ts`, `src/core/components/index.ts` | `drawShapeLayer` (`src/render/shapes.ts`), `applyPreDrawLayerFx` (`src/render/fx.ts`), `registerBuiltInComponents` (`src/core/components/index.ts`) | In `src/render/shapes.ts`, `case 'card':` hard-codes cast shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`, blur `32`, offsetY `14`), light sweep (`rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`), and stroke (`lineWidth = Math.max(2, strokeWidth)`). Other surfaces (`hero`, `disc`, `hdisc`, `warnp`) currently draw via `roundRect`/`ellipse` + `applyPreDrawLayerFx`. All 5 surfaces will route through `drawSurface` in `src/render/shapes.ts`. Built-in components (`chip`, `disc`, `badge`, `stamp`, `button`, `cursor`, `meter`, `bubble`, `chat`, `phone`, `browser`, `toast`, `kpi`, `timeline`, `logo`, `photo`) in `src/core/components/index.ts` use `style` tokens (`card`, `hero`, `disc`, `hdisc`, `warnp`) and token colors; only browser dots (`#FF5F57`, `#FEBC2E`, `#28C840`) and `#FFFFFF` on warn badge use hex literals (note: `chat` online dot `#28C840` is one of the 3 permitted literals). |
| **Macro & Preset Expansion** | `src/core/macros.ts`, `src/core/keyframes.ts`, `src/core/layout.ts` | `expandMacroToKeyframes(macro, layerStart, canvasW, canvasH, restW, restH, stylePack?)`, `evaluateLayerMotions`, `registerBuiltInMacros`, `normalizeEnterExit`, `evaluateEnterPreset`, `evaluateExitPreset` | `expandMacroToKeyframes` converts macro calls into keyframe tracks; `evaluateLayerMotions` evaluates `layer.resolvedMotions` in `renderFrame`. `evaluateEnterPreset` and `evaluateExitPreset` in `src/core/keyframes.ts` evaluate layer `enter`/`exit` presets. Compile-time motion modifiers (`overshoot`, `durScale`, `ease`, `enter`, `exit`, `timeQuantize`) will be applied when compiling/expanding macros and presets so `neon` (with `overshoot:1, durScale:1, ease:null, timeQuantize:null, enter:null, exit:null`) is a strict no-op. |
| **Camera Evaluation** | `src/core/layout.ts`, `src/render/camera.ts` | `compileRecipe` (`camera` array), `evaluateCamera(compiled, t)`, `applyCameraToContext` | `compileRecipe` builds `compiled.camera` from `doc.camera`. `pack.motion.camera` scales `zoom`, `x`, `y`, `rot` relative to key 0 and `shake` by `camera` at compile time. `pack.motion.slamShake` adds deterministic `(dx, dy)` in pixels after camera zoom in `evaluateCamera` / `applyCameraToContext` when `slamShake` is set and `pack.motion.camera > 1`. |
| **Text Measurement, Layout & Drawing** | `src/render/text.ts` | `resolveFontFamily`, `containsDevanagari`, `ensureFontsLoaded`, `measureAndLayoutText`, `drawTextLayer`, `drawHighlightLayer`, `drawCaptionsLayer` | Handles `fit: 'shrink'` binary search, prefix word boxes for `highlight`, `plate` background, `letterSpacing` (with Devanagari guard), `textAnim`, and `typeOn`/`charIn`/`wordIn` presets. Will be extended with `pack.text.case` (before measurement, skipping Devanagari), `pack.fonts` resolution, `pack.text.letterSpacing`, `pack.text.shadow`, `pack.text.outline` (`displayOnly` aware), and `pack.text.cursor` (blinking block cursor after `typeOn`). |
| **Shape Path Builders** | `src/render/shapes.ts` | `traceRoundedRect`, `traceRegularPolygon`, `traceStarOrBurst`, `drawShapeLayer`, `drawBurstRing` | Draws all 22 shape kinds centered at `(0,0)`. Will be extended with `drawSurface`, procedural cached textures (`paper`, `halftone`, `scan`), deterministic polyline flattening + normal displacement (`edge.wobble`, `freq`, `boil`) cached in a 300-entry LRU, `corner: 'sharp' \| 'round'`, `defaultStroke`, and `glow` policy (`allow`, `soften`, `off`). |
| **Background & Post Effects** | `src/render/background.ts`, `src/render/fx.ts` | `drawBaseSceneBackground`, `drawBackgroundLayer`, `drawGlobalFx`, `applyPreDrawLayerFx`, `applyPostDrawLayerFx` | `drawBaseSceneBackground` draws the scene backdrop (`flat`, `paper`, `halftone`, `crt`, `gridpaper`). `drawGlobalFx` applies post effects in composite order `bloom → chroma → scanlines → grain → vignette → flicker`, merging `pack.post` with per-key overrides from `doc.global` and seeding `grain` by frame index `i = Math.round(t * fps)`. |
| **Font Registration & Loading** | `src/render/text.ts`, `src/index.css`, `public/fonts/`, `src/assets/fonts/` | `ensureFontsLoaded(styleId?)`, `@font-face` declarations | Currently bundles `Sora`, `Inter`, `Noto Sans Devanagari`, `Noto Color Emoji` in `public/fonts/` and `src/assets/fonts/`. Will bundle subsetted `.woff2` files + OFL licences for `Baloo 2`, `Nunito`, `Bangers`, `Comic Neue`, `Kalam`, `JetBrains Mono`, `VT323`, and `Poppins`, and load the active pack's 3 fonts before first frame / export and lazily for style picker cards. |
| **Validator & Doctor Panel** | `src/core/validate.ts`, `src/ui/screens/RecipeEditorScreen.tsx` | `validateRecipe`, `estimateTextWidthPx`, `buildFixPrompt`, `validateStylePack` | `validateRecipe` returns `{ doc, issues, valid }` where `ValidationIssue` has `{ severity, code, message, layerId, sceneId, path, time, suggestedFix }`. Rendered in `RecipeEditorScreen.tsx` ("Validation Diagnostics"). Will add unknown `theme.style` error, `checkContrast` warnings, glow-in-no-glow-style warnings (capped at 10 + summary), active pack font width estimation, and `validateStylePack(json)` with JSON paths. |
| **Prompt Builder & Asset** | `src/core/promptbuilder.ts`, `src/assets/prompt-pack.txt`, `src/assets/promptPack.ts` | `buildPromptForPart`, `buildSceneChangePrompt`, `buildFixPrompt`, `PROMPT_PACK_TEXT` | `src/assets/prompt-pack.txt` and `src/assets/promptPack.ts` hold the master prompt text. `buildPromptForPart`, `buildSceneChangePrompt`, and `buildFixPrompt` will emit `STYLE: <id> (<name>)` and `STYLE GUIDE:` lines. `src/assets/style-pack-maker.txt` will be shipped verbatim. |
| **IndexedDB & Project Record** | `src/store/db.ts`, `src/store/projects.ts` | `StoredProject`, `AppSettings`, `IDB_STORE_NAMES`, `getDb`, `saveProjectToDb`, `loadAllProjectsFromDb` | `StoredProject` in `src/store/db.ts` stores project settings at top level (`theme`, `w`, `h`, `fps`, `screenText`). Will add optional `styleId?: string`, `embeddedStylePack?: StylePack`, and `settings?: { styleId?: string; embeddedStylePack?: StylePack }` plus the `stylepacks` IndexedDB store (`key = id`, `value = { pack, importedAt }`) and `mini:` thumbnail cache in IndexedDB. |
| **UI Screens & Navigation** | `src/ui/app.tsx`, `src/ui/screens/TranscriptPromptScreen.tsx`, `src/ui/screens/PreviewStageScreen.tsx`, `src/ui/components/ProjectDrawerModal.tsx` | `TranscriptPromptScreen` (theme/palette picker), `PreviewStageScreen` (stage toolbar), `ProjectDrawerModal` (Settings/Diagnostics & About/Licences) | Style picker with live `OffscreenCanvas` mini-renders attaches to `TranscriptPromptScreen.tsx` above the 5 accent swatches + hex input; **Style** (Restyle) sheet button attaches to `PreviewStageScreen.tsx` toolbar with non-blocking warning diff banner + Undo; **Styles** management tab (Import/Export/Delete/Copy Style Pack Maker Prompt) attaches to `ProjectDrawerModal.tsx`. |
| **Test Setup & Headless Rasterizer** | `package.json`, `src/render/goldenRaster.ts`, `tests/*.test.ts` | `node:test`, `node:assert/strict`, `createSoftwareCanvas2D(logicalW, logicalH, scaleFactor)` | Tests run via `tsx --test` (`node:test`). `createSoftwareCanvas2D` renders deterministic RGBA buffers and encodes RFC-2083 PNGs in `tests/goldens/`. |
